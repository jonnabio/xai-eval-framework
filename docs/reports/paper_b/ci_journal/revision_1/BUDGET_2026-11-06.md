# Budget of cohort B2 from the pilot (2026-11-06)

Pilot: every dataset × model × explainer, seed 11, 4 instances (1 per stratum), stability on
each; 175/175 jobs, 700 instance rows, **0 failed instances**; peak memory of any job 3.0 GB.
Full table: `pilot_budget.md` and `pilot_budget.csv` (from `scripts/paper_b_pilot_budget.py`).

## As configured: does not fit

- Quality pass: **349 CPU-hours**, ≥ 87 h of continuous running on 4 workers; longest job
  KernelSHAP on connect-4's SVM, 19.1 h.
- Timing pass: ≈ 36 h of quiet host, not overlapping the quality pass.
- Then WP4 (stability controls), WP5 (sweep), WP2b (RF vs XGBoost): not yet costed.

Where the time goes: by explainer, Anchors 150 h, SHAP 144 h, DiCE 51 h, LIME 2 h, LIME without
the feature budget 2 h. By model × explainer, **SVM × SHAP 143 h (41%)**, SVM × Anchors 65 h,
RF × Anchors 32 h, MLP × Anchors 30 h. KernelSHAP time per explanation on SVM: Adult 109 s,
connect-4 172 s, phishing 20 s, spambase 12 s. Stability (20 instances × 15 perturbations)
is about 75% of every job's explanation time.

## Recommendation (for the author's decision; nothing changed yet)

A + B, with C at night and at weekends:

- **A**: one explicit KernelSHAP budget, 500 coalitions with 50 background rows, for all
  non-tree models. It is stated in the paper. The sweep (WP5) covers the library default on a
  subset, so its effect is measured, not assumed. Even at 500, KernelSHAP evaluates ≈ 25,000
  model rows per explanation against LIME's 1,000, so it is not starved (R1-M4).
- **B**: stability on 12 instances per cell (3 per stratum) instead of 20, still with T = 15.
  The pilot shows stability to be the main cost driver. Twelve instances × 5 or 3 seeds × 5
  models per dataset keeps the stability comparison powered at the model level.
- Keep 100 instances per cell and 5 seeds on Adult (the primary benchmark).

Expected: quality pass ≈ 2 days of continuous running; timing pass ≈ 20 h (2–3 nights).
Schedule: quality 7–9 Nov, timing nights 9–11 Nov, sweep and controls 11–14 Nov, writing in
parallel; submission 30 Nov with margin.
